About Richard

A Disciple of Christ

I am a disciple of Christ and do not call myself a Christian. A Christian can mean many things today, but a disciple of Christ can mean one thing: you follow Jesus Christ. That is who I am.

Pleasing God, Not Man

Because that is true, I listen to what the Holy Spirit tells me, and I do it. That has got me into some conflicts with people, and yet, when I stood my ground on what the Holy Spirit told me, it caused a shift in my soul. I no longer sought to please man, but to please God.

A New Realm of Destiny

This has led me in the past decade into a new realm of my destiny. I wrote my first book in January 2025, My Letter to You, based on the Seven Letters to the Churches. This has been after over 45 years of teaching the Word of God. Then, I finished my second book in September 2026, Why the Rapture Tarries. I sense there is more to come, and so I will continue this journey with the Lord to do what He tells me to do.

Why I Write

More Than Information About God

For more than 45 years, I have had the privilege of teaching the Word of God. For more than 15 years, I have also had the privilege of discipling men coming from prison and drug rehabilitation.

Those years have taught me something important: people don't simply need more information about God. They need to discover what it means to follow Jesus Christ, grow in spiritual maturity, and live out the purposes of God.

That conviction is at the heart of my writing and ministry.

Discipling Men

Life, Hope, and Love

For nearly twenty years, I have been discipling men coming from prison and drug rehabilitation. This experience has marked me as I have witnessed miraculous transformations in men who came in one way and, six to nine months later, went out another way—men who now have life, hope, and love. Many are walking out their destiny.

“Men who came in one way and, six to nine months later, went out another way.”

The Message

Christ in You, the Hope of Glory

This has deeply affected the direction of my life. I have no other goal in life than to see the fruit of believers coming into the fullness of Christ through discipleship. As Paul wrote in Colossians 1:25-29, it bears witness in my soul.

“Of this Ekklesia, I have been made a disciple maker, according to the stewardship from God bestowed on me, for your benefit, so that I might fully carry out the teaching of God's Word, that is, the mystery that has been hidden in past ages and generations, but has now been manifest in the saints. To whom God willed to make known the riches of the glory of this mystery, which is Christ in you, the hope of Glory! We proclaim Christ!

Admonishing every man, teaching every man in wisdom, so that we present every man fully grown in Christ. For this purpose, I labor, striving according to His power that works mightily in me.”

Colossians 1:25-29
